# Piazza Cardinal Pacca: burial evidence and map reconstruction

Research checked 28 October 2026. This addresses the owner's recollection of
burials discovered a few years ago beneath modern Piazza Cardinal Pacca
(also called Piazza Santa Maria), in the city centre. The recollection of
burials at this location is supported by the 2023 excavation reporting.

## San Pietro and the other churches

The accessible excavation notice identifies **San Pietro delle Monache**, a
female Benedictine monastery with a church inserted into a Roman thermal hall.
It says the monastery is known from the eleventh century. It also names
**S. Jacobi a Foro** and **S. Stephani de monialibus de Foro**. The latter was
in the centre of today's square and was demolished in modern times to improve
access to the monastery. The modern open square results substantially from
1943 bombing; it must not automatically be copied as an empty AD700 plaza.

The companion church research records San Pietro delle Monache at approximately
**41.1317444 N, 14.7728028 E**, from the Wikipedia site entry, with its former
front toward Via San Gennaro. This is a church anchor, **not a surveyed 2023
grave-trench coordinate**. The owner calls the missing church “San Pietro ad
forum”; the exact equivalence of that wording to this convent has not been
established. Avoid creating two large San Pietro complexes in the same area
simply to satisfy both labels. Also do not confuse this urban site with the
existing San Pietro extra muros site across the Sabato.

## AD700 reconstruction decision

- **Attested at the stated location:** Roman thermal fabric and later churches;
  buried human remains/tombs found in 2023 inside the modern city centre.
- **Not established by these retrieved reports:** the date of each discovered
  grave, frescoed Lombard family catacombs, a traversable ancient gallery
  network, or the 2023 trench's exact geographic polygon and ancient level.
- **Authorized reconstruction hypothesis:** a compact burial group in/along
  reused Roman fabric in the Pacca/forum quarter, with modest slab/cist graves
  and a limited access space. A small early place of worship may be represented
  as a predecessor of later San Pietro, but the complete medieval convent plan
  must not be called documented for700.

For map integration, use the Pacca quarter north of the theatre and west of
the cathedral, coordinate the small church and burial space as one precinct,
and fit authored construction to existing registered lots. Do not move the
Triggio chamber here and call it the exact2023 discovery; they are distinct
locations. Do not place the Pacca burials outside the urban circuit merely
because earlier Roman cemeteries were often extramural.

The reported2m depth is below the **modern** surface and does not establish a
2m-deep chamber relative to the street in700. Burial depth, access, dimensions,
furnishings and painted decoration require reconstruction decisions. Keep
certainty labels and source limitations in development documentation; the
owner has asked for an uninterrupted in-game experience without hypothesis
badges.
