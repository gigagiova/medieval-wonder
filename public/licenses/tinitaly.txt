Terrain data: TINITALY 1.1
Tarquini S., Isola I., Favalli M., Battistini A., Dotta G. (2023).
TINITALY, a digital elevation model of Italy with a 10 meters cell size (Version 1.1).
Istituto Nazionale di Geofisica e Vulcanologia (INGV).
https://doi.org/10.13127/tinitaly/1.1
Source: https://tinitaly.pi.ingv.it/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modified for Medieval Wonder: cropped around Benevento, reprojected/resampled,
river channels adapted to the reconstruction, boundaries blended, heights
quantized to decimetres and local landmark terraces applied at runtime.
No endorsement by INGV is implied.

Earlier EU-DEM-derived terrain is retained at the crop boundary and in the
regional extension: Copernicus EU-DEM v1.1 via OpenTopoData, adapted through
smoothing and channel carving. Source documentation and access conditions:
https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/eudem/
