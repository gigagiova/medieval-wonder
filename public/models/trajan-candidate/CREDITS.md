# Experimental Arch of Trajan relief candidate

Photographic source: Bernard Gagnon (User:Bgag), “Arch of Trajan (Benevento) 03.jpg”,26 April2022.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arch_of_Trajan_(Benevento)_03.jpg
Dedicated by the photographer under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Derivatives: cropped narrative field; desaturation, large-scale illumination
flattening and contrast reduction; inferred relative depth compressed to a
shallow surface. This is not a measured scan. Current damage and some captured
shadows remain. Source and derivative provenance in
`docs/benevento/TRAJAN-RELIEF-ASSETS.md` and `TRAJAN-RELIEF-CANDIDATE.md`.

Offline depth inference: Depth Anything V2 Small, Apache2.0 model:
https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Small
ONNX conversion: https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/depth-anything-v2-small
https://depth-anything-v2.github.io/
Model weights and inference runtime are development tools, not distributed with
the browser application. No learned model runs on the player's phone.
