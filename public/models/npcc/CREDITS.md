# Townsfolk, track C (`?npc=c`): credits

Each person is one already-clothed model from Sketchfab, under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes made for the game (all of them):
rescaled to a period height, inner layers that are covered by outer ones removed, everything merged
into one mesh, decimated, the textures re-packed into one atlas (multi-part models) and converted to
.webp, a skeleton fitted and skinned automatically (tools/npcc/build.py), shields and swords removed
from the soldier.
